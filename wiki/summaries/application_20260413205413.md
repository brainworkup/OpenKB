# Summary: Apply to Y Combinator

## Overview
This document is a Y Combinator application page for **Joey Trampush**. It is a structured intake form listing the questions, requirements, and upload constraints needed to apply to YC. It does not contain substantive company answers; instead, it defines the application fields to be completed.

## Main Purpose
The document captures the information YC requests from applicants across:
- founder and technical background
- product description and demo
- location and logistics
- progress and tech stack
- market, competition, and revenue
- company history, legal status, and fundraising
- motivation for applying to YC

## Key Sections

### Team and Founders
Requests information about:
- who writes code or performs technical work
- whether any technical work was done by non-founders
- whether the applicant is seeking a cofounder
- a required one-minute founder introduction video

## Notable Requirements and Constraints
- Founder profile must be complete before submission.
- Founder intro video is required and must be under 100 MB.
- Demo video is optional but limited to 3 minutes / 100 MB.
- Coding agent session export is optional and limited to 25 MB in .md or .txt format.
- After submission, only certain sections remain editable: founder profiles, founder video, demo video, progress, and fundraising.

## Interpretation
The document shows how YC evaluates startups through a consistent set of lenses:
- founder capability and technical ownership
- clarity of product vision
- evidence of progress and user demand
- market understanding and differentiation
- legal/fundraising readiness
- motivation and fit for YC

It also indicates growing importance of [[concepts/ai-code-generation]] in startup execution by explicitly requesting AI tooling details and optionally allowing coding agent session exports.

## Summary Takeaway
This is a structured application template for YC that emphasizes founder quality, product clarity, traction, market insight, and increasingly, AI-enabled development workflows.
