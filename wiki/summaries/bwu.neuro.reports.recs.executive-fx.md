# Executive Functioning Recommendations (bwu.neuro.reports.recs.executive-fx)

This document provides clinician-generated recommendations for improving [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]] in a child (KB), targeting self-control, attention, time management, impulse control, and goal-setting.

## Key Recommendation Areas

### Self-Control and Impulse Regulation
- Provide **immediate reinforcement** for demonstrations of increased self-control or sustained attention.
- Praise effort and hard work rather than labeling KB as "smart" to encourage perseverance with difficult tasks.
- Use **[[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]]** training to help KB ignore distracting thoughts and improve impulse control.
- Practice **delay of gratification** to regulate impulses and support positive social interactions.
- Play structured "freeze" or clapping games to build inhibition of motor impulses, increasing complexity over time.

### Time Management and Sense of Time
- Allow KB to monitor his own progress using a **timing device** during clearly defined tasks.
- Have KB **estimate time** needed for specific activities to enhance his internal sense of time.
- Teach use of a **schedule or calendar** to record deadlines, appointments, and homework assignments.

### Goal-Setting and Task Completion
- Teach KB to set **realistic goals** and monitor progress toward them using [[concepts/goal-setting-self-monitoring]] strategies.
- Use a **contract approach** — developed collaboratively with KB — to foster independence and self-direction.
- Break **multistep assignments** into smaller, manageable steps with individual sub-goals.
- Teach and rehearse **problem-solving steps**; provide logical structures or procedures when possible.

### Responsibility and Independence
- Encourage KB to take responsibility for completing and submitting assignments on time.
- Establish and maintain **clear rules and expectations** around timeliness.
