# Young Adult ADHD — Recommendations

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.adhd.young-adult

A clinical recommendation template for young adults (particularly college students) with ADHD, covering medical treatment options, school tools, and home-based strategies.

## Medical Recommendations

- ADHD treatment typically combines **behavioral techniques**, **stimulant medication** (if indicated), **environmental organization**, and a long-term perspective on ADHD as a chronic condition.
- Referral to a prescribing physician for medication discussion is recommended.
- Lifestyle factors are emphasized as critical:
  - High-quality **sleep**
  - Balanced **nutrition**
  - Frequent, vigorous **exercise**

See also: [[concepts/adhd-treatment-recommendations]]

## School / Academic Tools

- **iA Writer** app is highlighted as an ADHD-friendly writing tool:
  - **Focus Mode** reduces the urge to re-read and tweak text (especially helpful with comorbid perfectionism).
  - **Reading Time** feature helps calibrate document length and avoid distraction.
- The document notes that tools benefiting people with ADHD tend to benefit all users, given that ADHD represents a spectrum rather than a binary diagnosis.

See also: [[concepts/iep-accommodations]]

## Home / Daily Life Strategies

### Attention & Listening

- **Active listening** skills are recommended to compensate for difficulty processing detailed verbal information:
  - Paraphrasing, clarifying, asking questions, giving nonjudgmental feedback.
- **External reminders**: calendars, alarms, written notes, and lists.

See also: [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]]

### Mindfulness

- [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]] training is recommended to develop cognitive control and ignore distracting thoughts.

### Box Breathing (Square Breathing)

- A structured breathing technique for concentration, mindfulness, and stress relief:
  1. Breathe in — 4 seconds
  2. Hold — 4 seconds
  3. Breathe out — 4 seconds
  4. Rest — 4 seconds

See also: [[concepts/box-breathing]]

### Social & Communication

- Social activities are strongly encouraged to foster positive socialization experiences.
