### Somatic Concerns
Preoccupation with physical health unusual even in clinical samples. Chronic fatigue/weakness, multi-system complaints (neurological, gastrointestinal, musculoskeletal). Item pattern consistent with conversion and somatization disorders.

### [[concepts/psychosis-clinical-features]]
Marked peculiarities in thinking and perception at a severity unusual even in clinical samples — likely hallucinations, magical thinking, possible delusional beliefs. Perfectionism, rigidity, ruminative decision-making difficulty, intolerance of change and contradiction, fear of own impulses.
