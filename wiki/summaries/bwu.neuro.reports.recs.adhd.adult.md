# Adult ADHD Recommendations

A curated list of recommended resources for adult [[concepts/adhd-clinical-features]] assessment and management, provided as part of a neuropsychological report recommendations set.

## Mindfulness Resources

- **[Mindfulness for Adult ADHD: A Clinician's Guide](https://www.guilford.com/books/Mindfulness-for-Adult-ADHD/Zylowska-Mitchell/9781462545001)** — By Lidia Zylowska and John T. Mitchell (Guilford Press). A clinician-oriented guide integrating [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]] practices with adult ADHD treatment. Relevant for both practitioners and informed patients.

## Additional Patient & Family Resources

- **[ADHD in Adults: A Guide for Patients and Families](https://www.additudemag.com/adhd-in-adults-a-guide-for-patients-and-families/)** (ADDitude Magazine) — Practical [[concepts/psychoeducation]] for adults with ADHD and their families.
- **[NIMH: ADHD](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/adhd-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder)** — National Institute of Mental Health overview of ADHD, covering diagnosis, treatment, and research.

## Key Themes

- [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]] as an intervention strategy for adult ADHD symptoms (e.g., attention regulation, impulsivity).
- [[concepts/psychoeducation]] for patients and families as a component of ADHD management.
- Integration of clinician-focused and patient-facing materials in neuropsychological report recommendations.
