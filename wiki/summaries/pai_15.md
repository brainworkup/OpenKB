## Clinical Features

### Broad Presentation
- Significant elevations across **multiple clinical scales**, suggesting broad psychopathology and likelihood of multiple diagnoses.
- Presentation consistent with **crisis state**: marked distress, depression, emotional lability, and anger.
- Pattern of **anxious ambivalence in relationships**: fear of abandonment vs. dependency; bitterness and resentment.
- **Hopelessness, resentment, and impulsivity** combine to elevate risk for self-harm; see [[concepts/suicide-and-violence-risk-indices]].

### Depression
- Primarily **cognitive features**: negative expectancies, low self-esteem, pessimism, worthlessness, hopelessness.
- Sadness and physiological disturbances play only a minimal-to-moderate role.
- See [[concepts/depression-clinical-features]] and [[concepts/major-depressive-disorder-clinical-features]].

### Personality Traits
- **Identity disturbance**: uncertain about major life issues, little sense of direction or purpose.
- **Impulsivity**: prone to self-harmful behaviors (spending, sex, substance use).
- **Antisocial character features**: marked need for stimulation/excitement, reckless behavior, thrill-seeking, unlikely to sustain long-term commitments.
- **Mood variability**: may view herself as active and ambitious; others see her as impatient and demanding.
- See [[concepts/borderline-personality-disorder]] and [[concepts/identity-disturbance-clinical-features]].

### Areas Denied / Not Elevated
- No unusual thoughts or peculiar experiences.
- No undue suspiciousness or marked anxiety.
- No significant reported problems with alcohol or drug abuse (though denial is flagged; see [[concepts/substance-use-clinical-assessment]]).
- No significant somatic/health difficulties.

## Self-Concept

- **Poorly established self-concept**; fluctuates between harsh self-criticism and relative self-confidence.
- Self-esteem tied to status of close relationships; feels incomplete and inadequate without relational anchoring.
- Self-esteem is **fragile** and plummets in response to interpersonal slights.
- Identity and attitudes about life shift with relational status.
- This pattern is characteristic of [[concepts/identity-disturbance-clinical-features]] as described in the PAI literature.
