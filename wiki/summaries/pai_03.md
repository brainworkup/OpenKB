## Clinical Features

### Primary Presentation
- Significant **unhappiness, moodiness, and tension**
- **Low energy, passivity, and social withdrawal** — may impede treatment engagement
- Very **low self-esteem**; feels ineffectual and powerless
- Difficulty concentrating and making decisions
- **Pessimistic** outlook; hopelessness and agitation elevate self-harm risk

### Anxiety
- Discomforting level of anxiety and tension
- Pervasive worry compromising concentration
- Somatic signs: sweaty palms, trembling, irregular heartbeats, shortness of breath
- Does not strongly report subjective tension or difficulty relaxing
- See [[concepts/anxiety-clinical-features]]

### Depression
- Primarily **cognitive features**: negative expectancies, worthlessness, hopelessness, personal failure
- Sadness and physiological disturbance are minimal to moderate

### Thought Processes
- Confusion, distractibility, poor concentration
- Possible tangential or circumstantial speech

### Obsessive-Compulsive Features
- Mild maladaptive behavior patterns aimed at controlling anxiety
- Perceived as perfectionistic; rigid adherence to personal guidelines
- Ruminates; difficulty making decisions; stressed by change or unexpected events
- Fears own impulses

### Mood Variability
- Elevated and variable mood; perceptibly high activity level
- Disorganized involvement in multiple activities; accelerated thought processes

### Alcohol Use
- Alcohol use has caused **occasional problems** (interpersonal, occupational, stress-coping)
- See [[concepts/substance-use-clinical-assessment]]

### Areas Denied
- No significant antisocial behavior, empathy deficits, suspiciousness/hostility, or somatic complaints
