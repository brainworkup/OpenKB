# Apply to Y Combinator

## Overview
This document is a largely unfilled YC application form for a Summer 2026 YC application associated with Joey Trampush. It captures the full set of questions YC asks founders about their company, product, traction, revenue, team, legal structure, fundraising, and motivation for applying.

## What the document contains
The application requests information across the standard startup evaluation areas:

- Founder technical contribution and whether any non-founders built the product
- Cofounder status and founder introduction video
- Company name, short description, URL, demo, and product link
- Product explanation and company location plans
- Progress to date, time spent, and full-time commitment
- tech stack details, including AI models and AI coding tools
- Optional export of a coding-agent session
- User traction, active users, paying customers, and top customer value
- revenue by month from November 2025 through April 2026
- Revenue sources and growth commentary
- Prior YC application history, pivots, and lessons learned
- Participation in incubators or accelerators
- Problem selection, domain expertise, and evidence of customer need
- founder evaluation and differentiation
- Business model and market-size/revenue potential
- Company category and alternate startup ideas
- Legal entity formation and equity ownership breakdown
- Prior investment, current fundraising, and reason for applying to YC
- How the applicant heard about YC and desired batch

## Notable features of this application version
### Emphasis on AI-enabled building
A notable addition is the explicit request for:

- AI models used in the product stack
- AI coding tools used by the founder
- An optional exported coding-agent transcript the founder is proud of

This suggests YC is evaluating not only what founders build, but also how effectively they use [[concepts/ai-code-generation]] and coding agents in product development.

### Standard startup diligence structure
The form reflects YC's recurring evaluation framework around:

- team quality
- speed of execution
- evidence of demand
- technical capability
- business model clarity
- founder insight
- ambition

## What is missing
The document does not include filled responses for most questions. As a result, it provides:

- the structure of the YC application
- the evaluation dimensions YC cares about
- the data founders are expected to provide

But it does **not** yet provide substantive facts about the actual company, product, traction, or finances.
