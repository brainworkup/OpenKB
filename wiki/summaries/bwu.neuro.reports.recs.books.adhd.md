# ADHD Books and Resources: Recommended Reading by Age Group

## Overview
This document provides a curated list of books, websites, and video resources related to [[concepts/adhd-clinical-features]] across the lifespan — from young children through older adults. Resources are organized by age group and context (school, college, etc.), serving as [[concepts/adhd-psychoeducational-resources]] for patients, families, and clinicians.

## Resources by Audience

### Children and Parents
- **The Family ADHD Solution** and **Mindful Parenting for ADHD** (developmentaldoctor.com) focus on parenting strategies.
- **ADHD 2.0** (Hallowell & Ratey) — covers childhood through adulthood.
- **Taking Charge of ADHD, 4th Ed.** (Russell Barkley) — comprehensive authoritative guide for parents.
- **12 Principles for Raising a Child with ADHD** (Russell Barkley) — practical parenting principles.
- **1-2-3 Magic** (Thomas Phelan) — effective discipline for children ages 2–12.
- **Learning to Slow Down and Pay Attention** (Nadeau & Dixon) — child-focused.
- **Thriving with ADHD Workbook for Kids** — 60 activities for self-regulation and focus.
- **Shelly the Hyperactive Turtle** (Deborah Moss) — picture book for young children.
- **Straight Talk About Psychiatric Medications for Kids** (Timothy Wilens) — medication guidance.

### Adolescents
- **Teenagers with ADD, ADHD, & Executive Functioning Deficits** (Chris Dendy) — covers [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]] in teens.

### School / Educators
- **All About ADHD: The Complete Practical Guide for Classroom Teachers** (Linda Pfiffner) — classroom management strategies relevant to [[concepts/adhd-classroom-interventions]].

### College Students
- **Coaching College Students with Executive Function Problems** (Mary T. Kennedy) — [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]] coaching in higher education.

### Young Adults
- **Launching in Young Adulthood with ADHD…Ready or Not!** (Chris Dendy) — transition to independence.

### Adults
- **Taking Charge of Adult ADHD** (Russell Barkley) — self-management for adults.
- **ADHD 2.0** (Hallowell & Ratey) — lifespan perspective.
- **The Adult ADHD Handbook for Patients, Family & Friends** — broad adult resource.
- **PsychCentral for Adult ADHD** — online resource hub.

### Older Adults
- **ADHD After Age 60** (ADDitude Magazine) — diagnosis and treatment in seniors; see also [[concepts/adhd-older-adults]].

### General / Uncategorized
- **The ADHD Advantage** (Dale Archer) — strengths-based perspective on ADHD.
- **On Task: How Our Brain Gets Things Done** (David Badre, PhD) — neuroscience of attention and [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]]; noted as particularly relevant for those interested in the field.
- **Chris Dendy Books** — multiple titles spanning adolescence and young adulthood.

## Online Resources
- [CHADD](https://chadd.org/) — major ADHD advocacy and information organization.
- [NIMH ADHD page](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/) — government mental health resource.
- [Child Mind Institute](https://childmind.org/) — child and adolescent mental health.
- [PsychCentral](https://psychcentral.com/) — adult ADHD resources.
