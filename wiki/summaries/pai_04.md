## Clinical Features

### Primary Concerns
- **Broad clinical elevation** across multiple scales — raises possibility of multiple diagnoses
- Significant distress centered on **physical functioning** and **depressive symptomatology**

### Depression
- Depressive symptoms elevated at a level **unusual even in clinical samples**
- Likely meets criteria for a **major depressive episode**
- Symptoms include: feelings of worthlessness, hopelessness, sadness, anhedonia, sleep disturbance, decreased energy, loss of appetite, psychomotor slowing
- Coefficient of fit with [[concepts/major-depressive-disorder-clinical-features]] profile: **.819** (highest among diagnostic groups)

### Somatic Concerns
- Unusual degree of concern about physical health and somatic symptoms
- Reports daily functioning compromised by numerous physical problems
- Frequent minor symptoms: headaches, pain, gastrointestinal complaints, fatigue
- Believes health is worse than age peers; views problems as complex and hard to treat
- Related rule-out diagnosis: [[concepts/somatic-symptom-disorder]]

### Thought and Perceptual Features
- Socially isolated with limited close relationships
- Limited social skills; difficulty interpreting interpersonal nuances
- No prominent active psychotic symptoms, but occasional concentration difficulties and unusual ideas noted
- See [[concepts/psychosis-clinical-features]] for context on schizophrenia rule-out

### Anxiety
- Worries significantly about issues beyond her control
- Anxiety impairs concentration and attention
- See [[concepts/anxiety-clinical-features]]

### Areas Without Significant Problems
- No antisocial behavior, undue suspiciousness/hostility, extreme moodiness/impulsivity, elevated mood
- No significant alcohol or drug use disorders
- See [[concepts/substance-use-clinical-assessment]]

- Suicide Potential Index: **68T**
- Critical items endorsed:
  - Item 100 (SUI): "I've made plans about how to kill myself" — *Very True (3)*
  - Item 340 (SUI): "I'm considering suicide" — *Mainly True (2)*
  - Item 206 (DEP-A): "I have no interest in life" — *Mainly True (2)*
- Level of Care Index: **81T** (elevated)
