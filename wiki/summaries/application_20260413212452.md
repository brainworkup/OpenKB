# Apply to Y Combinator

## Overview
This document is a mostly blank [[concepts/yc-application-planning]] form for applicant Joey Trampush. It captures the structure, questions, and submission requirements for a Y Combinator application rather than substantive company answers.

## Main Contribution
The document serves as a checklist of the information required to complete a YC application, including founder background, product description, traction, market, fundraising, and logistics.

## Key Sections

### Team and Founders
Requests information about:
- Who performs technical work
- Whether non-founders contributed technically
- Whether the applicant is seeking a cofounder
- A one-minute founder introduction video

### Product and Demo
Requests:
- A company description in 50 characters or less
- A fuller product explanation
- Product link and credentials
- Optional demo upload, capped at 3 minutes / 100 MB

### Location and Logistics
Requests:
- Current location and intended post-YC company location
- Reasoning behind location choice

### Progress and Tech Stack
Requests:
- Current product stage and progress
- Time spent working on the company, including full-time commitment
- Whether users are actively using the product
- Planned or current tech stack, including AI models and AI coding tools
- Optional exported coding-agent session transcript

### Strategy and Market
Requests:
- Why the founder chose the idea
- Relevant domain expertise and evidence of demand
- Competitor analysis and differentiation
- Revenue model and market size estimate
- Company category
- Notes on prior applications, pivots, and incubator participation

### Other Ideas and Legal
Requests:
- Alternative startup ideas considered
- Legal entity status
- Investment history
- Current fundraising status

### YC Specifics
Requests:
- Why the founder is applying to YC
- Whether anyone encouraged the application or whether the founder attended YC events
- How the founder heard about YC
- Desired batch timing

## Notable Constraints and Requirements
- Profile must be completed before submission
- Founder intro video required
- Founder video file size limit: 100 MB
- Demo file size limit: 100 MB and 3 minutes
- Coding agent export optional, max 25 MB, .md or .txt
- After submission, only limited sections remain editable

## Interpretation
This is best understood as an application template or intake form for [[concepts/startup-fundraising]], [[concepts/founder-evaluation]], and [[concepts/application-preparation]]. It reveals the categories YC uses to evaluate applicants: team quality, technical execution, product clarity, traction, market insight, and founder motivation.
