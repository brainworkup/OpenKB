# Aging — Book Recommendations

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.books.aging

## Overview

This document is a curated reading recommendation focused on [[concepts/aging]], listing a notable book on the topic of growing older with purpose and meaning.

## Recommended Book

### *The End of Old Age: Living a Longer, More Purposeful Life*
- **Author:** Marc Agronin
- **Link:** [marcagronin.com](https://www.marcagronin.com/book/the-end-of-old-age/)
- **Core Theme:** Reframing aging not as decline but as a stage of life with renewed purpose and meaning.

## Key Ideas

- Challenges conventional negative narratives around [[concepts/aging]].
- Proposes that longer life can be accompanied by greater purposefulness.
- Relevant to discussions of neurological health and longevity across the lifespan.
