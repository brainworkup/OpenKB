# Build Writing Skills: Recommendations for KB

## Overview
This document outlines clinical recommendations for KB, a student with identified difficulties in [[concepts/dysgraphia]], spelling, visual-motor coordination, and fine motor skills. The recommendations span instructional strategies, classroom accommodations, and referrals for additional evaluation.

## Core Areas of Difficulty
- Spelling and sight-vocabulary acquisition
- Written language complexity and composition
- Visual-motor coordination and spatial visualization
- Handwriting and fine motor speed
- [[concepts/dysgraphia]] impacting written output

## Instructional Strategies
- Enroll KB in an **evidence-based writing intervention** tailored to his weaknesses, with ongoing progress monitoring
- Practice spelling using **multiple modalities**: computer, chalkboard, plastic magnetic letters
- Use **games** involving letter manipulation to build spelling skills
- Develop a **personal problem-word list** of commonly misspelled words, updated regularly
- Practice **compound sentence construction** by combining simple sentences with conjunctions
- Enhance language development through **creative writing**: rewriting stories with synonyms or rhyming words

## Accommodations
- **Do not penalize** for misspelled words in non-spelling subjects; instead, use errors as teaching opportunities
- **Do not penalize** for poor handwriting given fine motor difficulties
- Allow KB to **dictate responses** to an adult on tests not directly measuring writing skills
- Allow **typed responses** on non-writing assessments to reduce fine motor demands
- Consider **speech-to-text software** as a supplement (not replacement) for writing instruction; see [[concepts/assistive-technology]]

## Referrals
- **Occupational therapy evaluation** is strongly recommended to address fine motor and gross motor challenges and improve task speed; see [[concepts/occupational-therapy]]
