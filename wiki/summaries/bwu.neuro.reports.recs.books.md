# Books Uncategorized — Recommended Reading

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.books

A curated list of book and online resource recommendations spanning neurology, aging, communication, and emotional regulation, likely compiled for patients, families, or clinicians.

## Communication

- **[Messages: The Communication Skills Book](https://g.co/kgs/EKmwBy)** — Appears in both the Communication section and the general list, suggesting it is a top recommendation for improving interpersonal communication skills.

## ADHD Resources

- **[ADHD 2.0](https://drhallowell.com/read/books-by-ned/)** — A modern guide to understanding and managing ADHD, authored by Dr. Ned Hallowell. Relevant to [[concepts/adhd-psychoeducational-resources]].
- **[The Adult ADHD Handbook for Patients, Family & Friends](https://www.amazon.com/Adult-Handbook-Patients-Family-Friends-ebook/dp/B004S3I8Z4)** — A practical resource targeting adults with ADHD and their support networks. See also [[summaries/bwu.neuro.reports.recs.adhd.books]].
- **[ADHD After Age 60](https://www.additudemag.com/adhd-in-seniors-diagnosis-and-treatment-after-60/)** — An online article addressing the diagnosis and treatment of ADHD in older adults, bridging [[concepts/adhd-older-adults]] and [[concepts/aging]].

## Aging

- **[In The End of Old Age: Living a Longer, More Purposeful Life](https://www.marcagronin.com/book/the-end-of-old-age/)** — A book by Marc Agronin focused on purposeful living in later life. Relevant to [[concepts/aging]] and [[concepts/purposeful-living]]. See also [[summaries/bwu.neuro.reports.recs.books.aging]].

## Emotional Regulation

- **[When Anger Hurts: Quieting the Storm Within](https://g.co/kgs/Tu3ieS)** — A resource on managing anger and reducing its harmful impact, relevant to [[concepts/anger-management]]. See also [[summaries/bwu.neuro.reports.recs.books.anger]].

- [[concepts/adhd-psychoeducational-resources]] — Multiple resources target ADHD across the adult lifespan, including in seniors.
