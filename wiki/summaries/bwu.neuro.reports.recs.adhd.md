# Autism Recommendations (BWU Neuro Reports)

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.adhd

## Overview

This document compiles autism intervention recommendations sourced from a cortex folder resource (`autism_resources.qmd`), originally written for a child. It notes the absence of adult-specific autism recommendations in the source repository and offers adapted guidance for adults.

## Key Behavioral Interventions

- **Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)** — The most empirically validated approach for ASD symptom treatment. Includes Pivotal Response Treatment (PRT), which leverages individual interests to boost motivation. See [[concepts/autism-intervention-strategies]].
- Finding ABA providers: Regional Centers, insurance networks, BACB.com, Autism Speaks resource guide.
- Strong ABA program markers: BCBA oversight, trained therapists, individualized assessments, data-driven progress tracking, family involvement.

## Social Skills Development

- **Structured Social Skills Groups** — Run by speech/language therapists or psychologists; discoverable via PsychologyToday.com.
- Focus areas: social conversation, perspective-taking, group interaction.
- May involve aides or one-to-one assistants.

## Structure and Routine

- Predictable routines for homework, socializing, meals, and sleep.
- **Visual supports**: timers, color-coded items, labels, posted checklists. See [[concepts/structured-teaching-teacch]].
- Consistency critical for managing transitions and expectations.

## Rules and Behavior Management

- Clear, written rules posted visibly.
- Organized reward systems (immediate experiences with parents) and consequences (time-out from reinforcing activities).
- Consistent implementation emphasized. See [[concepts/behavior-modification]].

## Parent Training and Support

- **Parent Training** — Behavior management using ABA or empirically-supported techniques.
- **Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT)** — Evidence-based for reducing challenging behavior and improving parent-child communication.

## Community Resources

- **Regional Center** — Services beyond insurance coverage: in-home behavioral services, speech/language therapy, occupational therapy, respite services, social skills treatment.
- **Autism Speaks 100 Day Kit** — Roadmap for community-based intervention.
- **Disability Rights California** — Advocacy.
- Recommended books from American Academy of Pediatrics and other experts.

## Adult-Specific Adaptations (Noted Gap)

No adult-specific autism recommendations were found in the source folder. Suggested adaptations include:

- ABA principles adapted for adult skill-building.
- Social skills groups targeting workplace and relationship contexts.
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) adapted for autism.
- Occupational therapy for sensory sensitivities and daily living skills.
- Vocational rehabilitation for workplace accommodations.
- Continued importance of structure and routine.

See [[concepts/autism-adult-support]] for cross-document synthesis of adult-focused resources.
