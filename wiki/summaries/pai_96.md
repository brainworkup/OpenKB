## Clinical Features

### Primary Presentations

- **[[concepts/major-depressive-disorder-clinical-features]]**: Significant depressive symptoms including worthlessness, hopelessness, anhedonia, loss of energy, sleep disturbance, psychomotor slowing, and decreased appetite. Consistent with a major depressive episode.
- **[[concepts/anxiety-clinical-features]]**: Significant anxiety and tension; excessive worry impairing concentration; physical symptoms (sweaty palms, trembling, irregular heartbeats, shortness of breath); difficulty relaxing.
- **[[concepts/identity-disturbance-clinical-features]]**: Uncertainty about major life issues; lack of sense of direction or purpose; instability in self-esteem tied to relationships.
- **Cognitive Difficulties**: Confusion, distractibility, difficulty concentrating; possible tangential or circumstantial speech. See [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]].
- **suicidal ideation** (plain text — no wikilink available): Recurrent suicidal thoughts; endorsed item #100 ("I've made plans about how to kill myself") at score of 1 (Slightly True); flagged as a significant warning sign.

### Areas Without Significant Elevation
Per self-report, no significant problems in: antisocial behavior, empathy deficits, undue suspiciousness or hostility, elevated mood or heightened activity, somatic/health difficulties, alcohol or drug abuse or dependence.

## Self-Concept

The respondent's self-concept is poorly established and characterized by harsh self-criticism and severe self-doubt. Self-perception varies as a function of close relationships; apart from identity established through relationships, he feels incomplete, unfulfilled, and inadequate. Self-esteem is fragile and plummets in response to perceived slights. This pattern is consistent with [[concepts/unstable-self-concept]] and [[concepts/fear-of-abandonment-and-rejection]].

## Interpersonal and Social Environment

Interpersonal style is modest, unpretentious, and retiring; self-conscious in social interactions; not skilled or comfortable asserting himself. Others likely view him as passive, humble, and unassuming. See [[concepts/social-avoidance-and-withdrawal]].

Recent stress level and perceived social support are both approximately average compared to normal adults — favorable prognostic indicators for future adjustment.

## Treatment Considerations

- **Suicidal Ideation**: Recurrent suicidal thoughts endorsed; warrants careful follow-up on ideation details, potential for action, life circumstances, and available support systems. See [[concepts/suicide-risk-assessment-pai]].

- **[[concepts/treatment-motivation-and-compliance]]**: Motivation is typical of individuals in treatment settings; higher than non-treatment adults. Acknowledges problems, perceives need for help, and reports positive attitude toward personal change and therapy.
- **Treatment Complication**: May currently be too disorganized or overwhelmed to participate meaningfully in some forms of treatment.
