# Brainworkup Branding Concepts

## Overview

This document presents seven distinct branding directions for **brainworkup.org**, a pediatric and forensic neuropsychology practice run by Dr. Trampush. Each concept addresses visual identity, tone, color palette, typography, letterhead design, and target audience. The concepts range from warm/parent-facing to highly academic/forensic, with one hybrid "dual identity" option.

## Concept Summaries

### Concept 1 · "Growing Minds" *(Warm & Supportive)*
- **Tagline:** *Understanding every mind at every stage*
- **Palette:** Teal (#2A9D8F), warm coral (#E07A5F), cream, charcoal
- **Visual:** Abstract brain silhouette with child profile; rounded sans-serif type
- **Tone:** Reassuring, parent-facing — "we see your child, not just their scores"
- **Best for:** Private clients, school referrals; less suited for forensic/legal audiences

### Concept 2 · "The Lighthouse" *(Warm & Supportive)*
- **Tagline:** *Clarity through assessment*
- **Palette:** Navy (#1D3557), amber (#F4A261), soft white, slate
- **Visual:** Lighthouse with light beam forming a neural pathway
- **Tone:** Empathetic and clear; accessible to both parents and attorneys
- **Best for:** Versatile across audiences — lighthouse metaphor works for both "finding answers" and "illuminating evidence"

### Concept 3 · "Clinical Modern" *(Mixed)*
- **Tagline:** *Pediatric & forensic neuropsychological assessment*
- **Palette:** Deep blue (#1B365D), clinical white, accent green (#4CAF50), light gray
- **Visual:** Minimal geometric brain icon; Inter or Söhne typography
- **Tone:** Professional, evidence-based — "Mayo Clinic meets a well-designed tech company"
- **Best for:** Hospital referrals, legal/forensic work, credential-seeking parents

### Concept 4 · "The Institute" *(Mixed — Leaning Academic)*
- **Tagline:** *Neuropsychological science in practice*
- **Palette:** Burgundy (#6B2737), gold (#C9A94E), ivory, charcoal
- **Visual:** Stylized "BWI" monogram in a shield/circle; secondary wordmark
- **Tone:** Authoritative; signals depth, research mission, and institutional rigor
- **Best for:** Legal, academic, hospital audiences; future-proofs if practice grows with associates or fellows

### Concept 5 · "Neural Blueprint" *(Mixed)*
- **Tagline:** *Mapping the developing mind*
- **Palette:** Blueprint blue (#1E3A5F), drafting gray, white, warm accent
- **Visual:** Architectural/schematic brain cross-section in monoline style
- **Tone:** Smart, distinctive; precision metaphor appeals to professionals
- **Best for:** Differentiation — stands out from generic neuropsych branding

### Concept 6 · "Academic Authority" *(Highly Academic)*
- **Tagline:** *Evidence-based assessment · Expert testimony · Clinical science*
- **Palette:** Oxford navy (#002147), gold (#D4A843), white, warm gray
- **Visual:** Modern crest/seal; serif wordmark (Freight or Merriweather)
- **Tone:** Unapologetically academic; CV as brand asset (USC, NIH, 20+ years)
- **Best for:** Forensic/legal referrals, hospital partnerships, IME work

### Concept 7 · "Dual Identity" *(Strategic Hybrid)*
- **Parent brand:** Brainworkup Institute
- **Sub-brands:** Brainworkup Pediatric (warm) + Brainworkup Forensic (authoritative)
- **Tagline:** *Neuropsychological assessment across the lifespan*
- **Visual:** One unified logomark in two color treatments
- **Website:** Forked entry — "I'm a parent" vs. "I'm an attorney or professional"
- **Best for:** Practices genuinely serving both audiences without compromise; most complex to implement

## Key Decision Factors

### "Institute" Naming — When It Makes Sense
- Want to signal more than a solo private practice
- Leverage USC faculty appointment and research publications as brand authority assets
- Forensic/legal work is a significant revenue stream (see [[concepts/dual-audience-design]])
- May bring on associates, postdocs, or psychometrists

### Keep "Brainworkup Neuropsychology" If
- Simplicity and personal connection are priorities
- Primary audience is parents who want to feel they're working with *a person*
- Avoiding overhead of institutional brand identity

### Middle Path
- Use **Brainworkup Institute** as the legal/brand entity
- Still introduce as "Dr. Trampush at Brainworkup" in parent-facing contexts

## Website Aesthetic Shift

The current [[concepts/quarto]] site has a data science aesthetic (dark themes, code blocks, monospace fonts). For a clinical practice, the recommended shift includes:

- **Light, airy backgrounds** — cream or white, not dark mode
- **Humanist or serif typography** — not monospace (see [[concepts/brand-typography]])
- **Clinical hero imagery** — children, families, abstract neuroscience (not terminal screenshots)
- **Clear CTAs** — "Schedule an Evaluation," "Referral Information"
- **Credential badges near top** — board certification, USC affiliation
- **Testimonial/outcome sections** — within HIPAA bounds

Quarto can remain the build tool with a custom clinical theme, or the site could migrate to Squarespace, Hugo, or Next.js for more design control. The [[concepts/brand-theming]] and [[concepts/brand-color-system]] choices made here would carry through whichever platform is selected.
