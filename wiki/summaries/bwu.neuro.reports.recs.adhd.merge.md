# BWU Neuro Reports: ADHD Recommendations (Merge)

## Overview
This document is a clinical recommendations template for individuals diagnosed with ADHD. It covers five major domains: medical/healthcare, school, home, work, and self-care, along with supplementary guidance on productivity strategies. The recommendations are described as synergistic — most effective when practiced together.

## Medical / Healthcare Recommendations
- **Multimodal treatment**: Combine behavioral techniques, stimulant medication, environmental organization, and a long-term chronic-condition perspective.
- **Pharmacotherapy**: Longer-acting stimulants (e.g., Vyvanse) preferred for lower misuse risk.
- **Psychotherapy**: Individual therapy targeting anxiety, stress, depression prevention; integrating [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]], distress tolerance, and [[concepts/cognitive-behavioral-therapy]].
- **Social skills training**: CBT-based approach to teach prosocial skills for real-world functioning.
- **Problem-solving skills**: Reframing situational conflicts as solvable dilemmas rather than hopeless situations.

## School Recommendations
- **Accommodated testing**: Extended time for high-stakes exams to address processing speed weakness.
- **Classroom recordings**: Lecture notes/slides provided in advance; audio/video recording permitted.
- **Preferential seating**: Minimize environmental distractions.
- **Academic advisement**: Regular advisor meetings for organization and deadline management.
- **Exam preparation**: Spaced, repeated exposure preferred over massed cramming; brief study intervals with reinforcement.

See also: [[concepts/adhd-classroom-interventions]], [[concepts/iep-accommodations]].

## Home Recommendations
- **Writing tools**: iA Writer (distraction-free, markdown-based) recommended for writing assignments.
- **Reminders and lists**: Calendars, alarms, written notes for task memory support.
- **Mindfulness**: Ignoring distracting thoughts; developing cognitive control via [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]].
- **Lifestyle foundations**: High-quality sleep, balanced nutrition, vigorous exercise.
- **Active listening**: Paraphrasing, clarifying, asking questions to compensate for verbal processing difficulties.
- **Attention/executive functioning**: Multimodal information presentation; digital planners; written checklists; compensatory skills. See [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]].
- **Processing speed**: Flashcard drilling with graduated fact introduction and color-coding by subject.
- **Fluid reasoning**: Pattern identification, story analysis, video gaming (cautiously) as practice.
- **Cognitive flexibility**: Practicing flexible thinking to reduce rigidity; self-talk technique for problem-solving (state problem → 3 solutions → choose one). See [[concepts/cognitive-flexibility-training]].
- **Recommended books**: *Taking Charge of Adult ADHD*, *ADHD 2.0*, *Messages: The Communication Skills Book*.
- **Recommended apps**: Todoist, Morgen, Gingkoapp, Shortform, Textsniper.

## Work Recommendations
- **Flexible schedule**: Adjust start/end times, allow short breaks, work during peak alertness hours.
- **Protected note-completion time**: Uninterrupted blocks for focused task completion.
- **Speech-to-text software**: Dragon, Apple Dictation, Google Docs voice typing, SpeechTexter, Gboard.

## Self-Care Strategies
1. Brief, focused study sessions with a timer; app recommendation: Motion (AI calendar/task planner).
2. Study during personal peak productivity times (morning or post-exercise).
3. Low-distraction, uncluttered workspace.
4. White noise or instrumental music to mask auditory distractions.
5. Reminders and lists.
6. Sleep, nutrition, exercise fundamentals.
7. **Box Breathing** (4-4-4-4 seconds): inhale → hold → exhale → rest; supports concentration, mindfulness, and stress relief.

## Productivity Frameworks
### Deep Work (Cal Newport)
- Focus without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks.
- Key tactics: avoid social media, embrace boredom, stop task-switching, sustain single-task focus.
- Linked to [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]] and attention management.

### Eisenhower Matrix (Priority Setting)
- Four quadrants: important+urgent, important+non-urgent, unimportant+urgent, unimportant+non-urgent.
- Resist responding to immediacy over importance.

### Tackle Toughest Task First
- Leverage morning energy before decision fatigue sets in.

## Group CBT Protocol (Reference)
Six therapeutic goals based on established CBT programs:
1. **Psychoeducation**: ADHD awareness and Beck's cognitive model.
2. **Parent training**: Routines, rewards, predictable environments.
3. **Organizing and planning**: Daily routines, task decomposition.
4. **Problem-solving**: Identifying problems, generating solutions, evaluating consequences.

## Summary Note (Georgia)
Benefits from structured routine, clear instructions, task breakdown, visual planners, distraction minimization, metacognitive strategy training ([[concepts/working-memory]]), emotional regulation ([[concepts/cognitive-behavioral-therapy]]/stress management), and multidisciplinary support (occupational therapist, behavior therapist).
