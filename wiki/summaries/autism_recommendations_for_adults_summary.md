# Autism Recommendations for Adults (and Children) — Summary

## Overview

This document compiles recommendations from cortex files (primarily `autism_resources.qmd`) for individuals with autism spectrum disorder. The source material is primarily pediatric-focused, but the document extracts and adapts principles for adults and identifies gaps in adult-specific guidance.

---

## Core Intervention Areas

### 1. Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)

- Most empirically validated approach for autism spectrum disorder symptoms
- Includes **Pivotal Response Treatment** (uses individual interests to boost motivation)
- Focus areas: aggressive behavior, language, communication, social interaction
- Programs must be designed by a Board-Certified Behavior Analyst
- Key components: individualized assessment, data tracking, family involvement, generalization across environments
- ABA principles can be adapted for adult skill-building
- See [[concepts/autism-intervention-strategies]]

### 2. Social Skills Development

- Structured social skills groups run by speech-language therapists or psychologists
- Emphasis on social conversation, perspective-taking, and group interactions
- Social stories for teaching appropriate behavioral responses
- Adult social skills groups exist for workplace and relationship contexts
- See [[concepts/autism-intervention-strategies]]

### 3. Structure and Routine

- Predictable daily schedules are critical for success
- Transitions and changes to routine can cause significant problems
- Practical tools: timers, checklists, color-coding, visual schedules
- Structured teaching approaches (e.g., TEACCH) align with these principles — see [[concepts/structured-teaching-teacch]]
- Routine remains important for adults with autism

### 4. Behavioral and Emotional Support

- Clear written rules and expectations posted visibly
- Reward systems: immediate, relational rewards preferred over tangible ones
- Consequences target behavior, not the individual
- Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) adapted for autism recommended for adults
- Mindfulness and stress management for emotional regulation
- See [[concepts/autism-adult-support]]

### 5. Parent and Family Support

- Parent Training with psychologist or ABA provider
- **Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT)**: evidence-based, reduces challenging behavior, improves communication
- Family members trained to reinforce skills and participate in planning
- Resources: Autism Speaks 100 Day Kit, Disability Rights California

### 6. Community-Based Services

- **Regional Center** (California): access services not covered by insurance
  - In-home behavioral services, speech/language therapy, occupational therapy, respite, social skills treatment
- Autism Speaks resource guide and BACB provider locator

## Adult-Specific Recommendations

No dedicated adult autism resources were found in the source cortex files. Adaptations and additions identified include:

| Domain | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Therapy | CBT adapted for autism (CBT-A), ACT for autism |
| Workplace | Structured environment, written communication, job coaching, flexible scheduling |
| Independent Living | Life skills training (budgeting, cooking), supportive housing |
| Social | Adult interest-based groups, community involvement, volunteer opportunities |
| Sensory | Occupational therapy for sensory sensitivities, noise-canceling tools |
| Vocational | Vocational rehabilitation services, workplace accommodations |
| Mental Health | Regular therapy, mindfulness, relationship counseling |

See [[concepts/autism-adult-support]] for cross-document synthesis on adult-oriented supports.

## Assessment Context

The file `bwu.neuro.reports.nt.acs.social-cognition.md` contains social cognition assessment measures (not treatment recommendations) covering:
- Social-emotional understanding
- Emotional expression and regulation
- Verbal and nonverbal communication
- Adaptation to change / restricted interests

These assessment domains relate to [[concepts/autism-diagnostic-rating-scales]] and [[concepts/autism-spectrum-disorder-clinical-features]].
