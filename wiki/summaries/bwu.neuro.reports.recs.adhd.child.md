# ADHD Child: Clinical Recommendations

## Overview
This document provides structured clinical recommendations for a child diagnosed with [[concepts/adhd-clinical-features]] (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder), covering medical/health care, school accommodations, home strategies, and reading resources. The child also presents with comorbid dyslexia and challenges in [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]].

## Medical / Health Care Recommendations
- ADHD treatment typically combines **behavioral techniques**, **stimulant medication** consideration, **environmental organization**, and a long-term chronic-condition perspective.
- A **methylphenidate preparation** at low-to-medium dosage may be appropriate for addressing inattentiveness and impulsiveness.
- Referrals recommended for:
  - **Educational therapist** (school demands, reading, attention/executive difficulties) — see [AET](https://www.aetonline.org/)
  - **Speech/language evaluation** (articulation and language development) — see [[concepts/speech-language-development-disorders]]
  - **Occupational therapy** (fine/gross motor development and executive functioning)

## School Recommendations
Key themes across 19 specific recommendations:

### Formal Accommodations
- Initiate **IEP evaluation** by sharing the neuropsychological report with school — see [[concepts/iep-accommodations]].
- **Extended time** in a distraction-free environment for tests and standardized exams (1.5× or 2×).
- Testing in a **small, structured setting** outside the classroom.

### Attention & Organization Supports
- Assign a **skills trainer/monitor** as liaison between student, teachers, and parents; regular check-ins at beginning and end of each week.
- Use a **study buddy** for note-sharing and homework clarification.
- Use of a **day planner or calendar**; anticipatory guidance for upcoming assignments.
- Cue the student 10 minutes before end of class periods.
- **Classroom seating**: minimally distracting placement; front of class or best position for focus.

### Instructional Strategies
- Break assignments into **shorter, sequentially organized steps** with feedback at each stage.
- Emphasize **"big picture" thinking** before detail work; ask the student to restate task goals.
- Provide **auditory and visual** (multimodal) presentation of material; allow recording of lectures.
- Vary **instructional medium and pace**; favor hands-on, active learning.
- Schedule **most demanding tasks in the morning**; allow frequent movement breaks.
- Reduce large assignments: assign every third problem, give one sheet at a time.
- Ensure **eye contact** during instruction; ask student to paraphrase directions.
- Emphasize **accuracy over speed**; provide separate grades for accuracy and speed.
- Teach work-checking strategies (before and after completion).
- Reduce homework load; focus on guided practice of mastered material.
- Provide **external organization**: previewing tasks, real-time commentary, and post-task review.

### Verbal Instruction
- Be explicit; confirm comprehension by having the student repeat commands.
- Use praise and positive reinforcement following proper execution.

## Home Recommendations
- Parents encouraged to learn about ADHD via [CHADD](https://chadd.org/) and [NIMH](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/) — see also [[concepts/adhd-resources-by-lifespan]].
- **Structured study environment**: short intervals with reinforcement, free of clutter, phone off during study.
- **Mindfulness** training to develop cognitive control and reduce distracting thoughts — see [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]] and [Mindful Parenting for ADHD](https://developmentaldoctor.com/mindful-parenting-adhd/).
- Encourage **extracurricular activities** (balanced with academics) for socialization and creative success.
