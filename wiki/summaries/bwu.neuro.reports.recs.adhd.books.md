# ADHD Books: Recommended Reading List

## Overview
A curated list of book recommendations related to [[concepts/adhd-clinical-features]] organized by audience and life stage. Intended as a resource guide, likely distributed alongside neuropsychological or clinical assessment reports. See also [[concepts/adhd-resources-by-lifespan]] for cross-document synthesis of ADHD resources by developmental stage.

## Books by Category

### For Parents of Children with ADHD
- **Learning to Slow Down and Pay Attention** — Kathleen G. Nadeau & Ellen B. Dixon
- **1-2-3 Magic: Effective Discipline for Children 2-12** — Thomas W. Phelan
- **Taking Charge of ADHD, 4th Ed.** — Russell Barkley; comprehensive authoritative parent guide

### Books for Children
- **Shelly the Hyperactive Turtle** — Deborah Moss; accessible for young children
- **Straight Talk About Psychiatric Medications for Kids** — Timothy E. Wilens, M.D.; covers psychiatric medication considerations for pediatric populations

### Adolescence
- **Teenagers with ADD, ADHD, & Executive Functioning Deficits** — Chris Dendy; addresses [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]] challenges in teens

### School Context
- **All About ADHD: The Complete Practical Guide for Classroom Teachers** — Linda J. Pfiffner; targeted at educators

### Young Adulthood
- **Launching in Young Adulthood with ADHD… Ready or Not!** — Chris Dendy; transition and independence skills

### Adult ADHD
- **Mindfulness for Adult ADHD: A Clinician's Guide** — Zylowska & Mitchell; [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]]-based clinical approach
- **Taking Charge of Adult ADHD, Second Edition** — Russell Barkley; strategies for work, home, and relationships

### General / Uncategorized
- **Chris Dendy Books** (collection) — multiple titles across age groups
- **Messages: The Communication Skills Book** — general communication skills resource

## Key Themes
- Resources span the full developmental lifespan: childhood through adulthood, consistent with [[concepts/adhd-resources-by-lifespan]]
- Multiple Russell Barkley titles reflect his prominence in ADHD research and treatment
- Chris Dendy titles recur across adolescent and young adult categories
- Includes both clinical guides (for professionals/parents) and child-facing books
- Touches on [[concepts/executive-function-deficits]], psychiatric medication for children, [[concepts/mindfulness-based-interventions]], and school-based support
