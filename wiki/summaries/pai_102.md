The protocol is complete (0 missing items). Consistency indicators suggest adequate attention to item content. However, **mixed validity concerns** are present:

- **Negative impression management** indicators are elevated (Malingering Index T=84; Hong Malingering Index T=73; Multiscale Feigning Index T=70), suggesting a tendency to portray himself in a consistently negative/pathological manner. Results are not rendered invalid but should be interpreted cautiously — clinical elevations may overrepresent pathology.

- Response set fit analysis shows moderate fit to both PIM (0.756) and NIM (0.746) predicted profiles, reflecting the mixed impression management picture.
