## Validity of Test Results

The protocol shows **mixed validity indicators** — a notable finding in this report:
- **Missing items:** Within acceptable limits (0 missing items)
- **Consistency:** Adequate attention to item content, but some idiosyncratic responses noted
- **Positive impression management (defensiveness):** Mild tendency to present favorably; not sufficient to invalidate results but may **underrepresent** symptom severity in some areas
- **Negative impression management (feigning):** Subtle indicators suggest possible exaggeration of pathology in certain areas; patterns resemble those seen in individuals feigning mental disorder; may **overrepresent** severity in other areas
- The profile has a significant coefficient of fit with the NIM predicted profile (.650) and Fake bad (.549), as well as Motor vehicle accident claimants (.708)

## Clinical Features

### Primary Presentation
- **Prominent hostility and suspiciousness** with hypervigilance; questions motives of others
- **Acute tension and fearfulness**; hypersensitive in social interactions
- **Phobic behaviors** likely interfering with functioning; possible agoraphobia or multiple phobias
- **Perfectionism and rigidity**: inflexible personal conduct guidelines, difficulty with decisions, rumination
- **Significant somatic concerns**: physical complaints involving neurological and musculoskeletal systems; possible conversion disorder features (unusual sensory/motor dysfunction); social interactions focused on health
- **Mild depressive symptoms**: sadness, anhedonia, loss of interest
- **Anxiety within normal range** (general worry)
- **Intense, volatile relationships** with fears of abandonment/rejection (borderline feature)

### Areas With No Significant Problems Reported
- Unusual thoughts / peculiar experiences
- Antisocial behavior
- Problems with empathy
- Elevated mood or heightened activity
- Alcohol or drug abuse/dependence
