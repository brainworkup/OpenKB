# Behavior Modification Recommendations

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.behav-mod

## Overview
This document provides a set of clinical recommendations for [[concepts/behavior-modification]] targeted at patients and their families. The recommendations emphasize shifting from negative/punitive interactions toward self-awareness, clear expectations, and positive reinforcement.

## Key Recommendations

### 1. Self-Monitoring
- The patient is encouraged to monitor their own behavior.
- Benefits include:
  - Creating opportunities for family discussion when perceptions of behavior severity differ.
  - Promoting an **internal frame of reference** in the patient for evaluating their own behavior.
- Supports development of [[concepts/self-monitoring-behavior]] skills.

### 2. Offering Alternatives to Criticism
- Instead of only pointing out inappropriate or undesirable behaviors, family members should also identify **available alternatives**.
- Clarifies expectations for the patient.
- Reduces the patient's perception of constant criticism.

### 3. Praise and Positive Reinforcement
- In addition to identifying undesirable behaviors, family members should **praise appropriate or desirable behaviors**.
- Reduces the overall frequency of negative statements in family interactions.
- Addresses and mitigates the patient's experience of criticism as relentless or pervasive.

## Themes
- [[concepts/behavior-modification]]: Core framework underlying all three recommendations.
- [[concepts/family-dynamics]]: Recommendations are directed at both patient and family unit.
- [[concepts/self-monitoring-behavior]]: Encouraging internal evaluation as a long-term skill.
- [[concepts/treatment-motivation-and-compliance]]: Recommendations are designed to support engagement and reduce resistance.
