# Conversion/Somatic Symptoms — Treatment Recommendations

**Source:** bwu.neuro.reports.recs.conversion

## Overview

This document outlines first- and second-line treatment recommendations for patients presenting with [[concepts/conversion-disorder]] or [[concepts/somatic-symptom-disorder]].

## Treatment Hierarchy

### First-Line: Education
- The primary initial intervention is **patient education** about the nature of conversion and somatic symptom disorders.
- Educating patients about their condition is foundational before escalating to other therapies.

### Second-Line: Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
- For cases that **do not respond to education alone**, [[concepts/cognitive-behavioral-therapy]] (CBT) is recommended as the next step.
- CBT is the evidence-based psychological intervention of choice for these disorders.

## Resources
- Patient-facing educational materials can be found and printed at: [www.neurosymptoms.org](http://www.neurosymptoms.org)
- This resource supports the education-first approach by providing accessible information for patients.
