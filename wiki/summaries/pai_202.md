## Validity of Test Results

The protocol shows **mixed validity indicators**:
- Completion: Acceptable (0 missing items)
- Attendance to content: Adequate, but some idiosyncratic responses noted
- Response style: **Dual distortion pattern** — both defensive (denial of substance use) and negative impression management (endorsement of bizarre/unlikely symptoms)
- Negative distortion indicators within acceptable range but elevated (e.g., Multiscale Feigning Index T=79, Hong Malingering Index T=72)
- Profile fits NIM predicted profile (0.895) and "fake bad" profile (0.852) at statistically significant levels
- Results should be interpreted **cautiously**; clinical elevations likely **overrepresent** psychopathology in some areas
- Possible interpretations of negative responding: "cry for help," careless responding, or extremely negative self-evaluation

See [[concepts/validity-and-response-styles]] and [[concepts/malingering-detection]] for cross-document context on these patterns.

## Clinical Features

### Substance Use
- Drug use problems **above average even for specialized treatment populations**
- Likely substance dependence: loss of control, probable withdrawal symptoms
- Denial of alcohol/substance use relative to personality-predicted levels
- Drug use implicated in interpersonal, legal, vocational, financial, and medical impairment
- See [[concepts/substance-use-clinical-assessment]] for related assessment context

### Depression
- Significant depressive features with feelings of worthlessness, hopelessness, personal failure
- Loss of interest (anhedonia), sadness reported
- Relatively **few physiological signs** (energy, appetite, sleep relatively intact)
- Depression and drug use likely **bidirectionally related**
- See [[concepts/depression-clinical-features]] and [[concepts/major-depressive-disorder-clinical-features]]

### Psychotic/Thought Features
- Social isolation and detachment; limited social skills
- Thought confusion, distractibility, concentration difficulties
- **Active psychosis (hallucinations/delusions) NOT prominent** at this time
- One critical item: hearing voices (SCZ-P, item 170, ST)
- See [[concepts/psychosis-clinical-features]]

### Borderline Personality Features
- History of intense, volatile relationships; fears of abandonment/rejection
- Unstable identity; little sense of direction or purpose
- Impulsivity (spending, sex, substance use)
- Elevated risk for self-mutilation and suicidal behavior
- Fragile, fluctuating self-concept
- See [[concepts/borderline-personality-disorder]], [[concepts/fear-of-abandonment-and-rejection]], and [[concepts/identity-disturbance-clinical-features]]

### Anxiety and Trauma
- Significant anxiety and tension; somatic signs (sweaty palms, trembling, palpitations, shortness of breath)
- **Phobic avoidance** (possible agoraphobia or multiple phobias)
- Evidence of posttraumatic stress: reliving traumatic events, avoidance, sustained distress
- Traumatic stressors endorsed across three critical items (ARD-T)
- See [[concepts/anxiety-clinical-features]] and [[concepts/trauma-informed-clinical-assessment]]

### Somatic Symptoms
- Unusual concern about physical functioning
- Frequent minor physical complaints (headaches, pain, GI problems), fatigue
- Pattern consistent with somatization
- See [[concepts/somatic-symptom-disorder]]

### Paranoid/Antagonistic Traits

- Attributes misfortunes to others' neglect; envious
- Passive-aggressive interpersonal style
- See [[concepts/paranoia-and-suspiciousness]]

### Areas Without Significant Problems
