## Clinical Features

### Anxiety and Trauma
- Reports **extreme anxiety** unusual even in clinical samples; life severely constricted by tension.
- Trauma indicators strongly present: reliving a disturbing traumatic event, recurrent anxiety episodes, loss of interest in previously enjoyed activities. See [[concepts/trauma-informed-clinical-assessment]].
- Likely phobic behaviors (possibly agoraphobia or multiple phobias); hypervigilant monitoring of environment.
- Cognitive impairment from worry affects concentration and attention.
- [[concepts/anxiety-clinical-features]] are broadly elevated across cognitive, affective, and somatic domains.

### Depression
- Significant [[concepts/major-depressive-disorder-clinical-features]] present: worthlessness, hopelessness, anhedonia, sadness, sleep disturbance, low energy, psychomotor slowing, appetite/weight changes.
- Persistent depressive features also noted; see [[concepts/persistent-depressive-disorder]].

### Paranoia and Hostility
- Marked suspiciousness and hypervigilance; questions motives of others. See [[concepts/paranoia-and-suspiciousness]].
- Extremely sensitive; holds grudges; quick to perceive inequitable treatment.
- Working relationships likely strained; perceived as hostile by others.
- Critical item endorsement: believes he is the target of a conspiracy (PAR-P, item 309).

### Somatic Concerns
- Unusual degree of concern about physical health; daily functioning compromised by varied physical complaints (headaches, pain, GI problems, fatigue).
- Self-image influenced by belief of being handicapped by poor health.
- Consistent with [[concepts/somatic-symptom-disorder]] features.

### Personality Features
- History of intense, volatile relationships; fear of abandonment/rejection. See [[concepts/fear-of-abandonment-and-rejection]] and [[concepts/borderline-personality-disorder]].
- [[concepts/social-avoidance-and-withdrawal]]: few close, warm relationships; social isolation and detachment.
- [[concepts/unstable-self-concept]] features evident in self-report.
- Adventurousness, risk-taking, impulsivity noted.
- Passive-aggressive personality features considered in differential.

### Substance Use
- Self-reports NO significant alcohol or drug problems.
- However, profile characteristics suggest possible denial of substance use — caution advised. See [[concepts/substance-use-clinical-assessment]].
- Critical item: "I have trouble controlling my use of alcohol" (ALC, item 55, ST).
- Related: [[concepts/alcohol-use-disorder-clinical-features]].

## Treatment Considerations

### Suicide Risk — CRITICAL
- Reports **intense and recurrent suicidal thoughts** at a level typical of individuals placed on suicide precautions.
- Suicide Potential Index T=93; Chronic Suicide Risk Index T=83.
- **Immediate evaluation and intervention required.**
- Risk heightened by: situational stressors, social isolation, lack of social support.
- See [[concepts/suicide-risk-assessment-pai]] for interpretive framework.
- Critical item endorsements:
  - "I've made plans about how to kill myself" (SUI item 100, VT=3)
  - "I'm considering suicide" (SUI item 340, VT=3)
  - "I have no interest in life" (DEP-A item 206, ST=1)

### Anger
- Describes temper as within normal range and fairly well-controlled.
- However, critical items flag aggression: threatens to hurt people (AGG-P, MT), loses control of temper (AGG-P, MT).

### Treatment Motivation
- Motivation typical of treatment-seeking adults; acknowledges problems and need for help.
- Positive attitude toward therapy and personal change. See [[concepts/treatment-motivation-and-compliance]].
- **Treatment complications anticipated:**
  - Trust issues with treating professional.
  - May feel too overwhelmed/disorganized to engage meaningfully.
  - May resist psychological explanations for problems.
  - Impasses in therapeutic relationship require careful management.
