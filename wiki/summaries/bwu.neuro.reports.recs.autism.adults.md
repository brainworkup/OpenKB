# Autism Recommendations (bwu.neuro.reports.recs.autism.adults)

A compiled reference document summarizing recommendations for Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) drawn from neuropsychological reports and cortex resources. The primary source material is pediatric-focused (referencing a child called "Biggie"), but the document explicitly notes adult applicability and includes a section on adult-specific adaptations.

## Key Finding

No adult-specific autism recommendations were found in the cortex folder at time of authoring. The document flags this gap and suggests adding adult-focused content. Many pediatric principles are adaptable to adults.

## Behavioral Interventions

- **Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA)** — Most empirically validated approach for ASD; should be designed/monitored by a Board-Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA). See [[concepts/autism-intervention-strategies]] for broader context.
  - Pivotal Response Treatment: leverages individual interests to increase motivation
  - Requires detailed individualized assessment, data tracking, family training, and regular team meetings
  - Resources: [BACB.com](http://www.bacb.com), [Autism Speaks Resource Guide](https://www.autismspeaks.org/resource-guide), Kennedy Krieger Institute
- **Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT)** — Evidence-based; reduces challenging behavior, improves parent-child communication
- **Parent Training** — ABA-based behavior management strategies; locate via [PsychologyToday.com](http://www.psychologytoday.com)

## Therapeutic Approaches

- **[[concepts/social-skills-training]]** — Structured groups led by speech/language therapists or psychologists; targets social conversation, perspective-taking, group interaction
- **Speech and Language Therapy** — Functional communication and social language; see [[concepts/speech-language-development-disorders]]
- **Occupational Therapy** — Sensory processing, fine motor skills, daily living skills
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)** — Adapted for autism; recommended for adults specifically

## Home and School Support

### Structure and Routine
- Predictable daily schedules for homework, meals, socializing, sleep
- Timers throughout the home for activity transitions
- Visual schedules, color-coded items, labels, posted checklists
- Organized physical environments with designated spaces

### Behavior Management
- Clear, simple, written rules posted visibly
- Consistent rewards (experiential, not tangible) and consequences
- Consequences target behavior, not the child (e.g., time-out from reinforcing activities)
- Positive reinforcement system emphasizing bonding activities; see [[concepts/behavior-modification]]

### Academic Accommodations
- Individualized Education Plan (IEP) or 504 Plan; see [[concepts/iep-accommodations]]
- Extended time for tests and assignments
- Assistive technology (keyboarding, voice-to-text)

## Community Resources (California/LA-focused)

- **Regional Center Services** — Access via [DDS Regional Center](http://www.dds.ca.gov/RC/index.cfm); covers in-home behavioral services, speech/language therapy, OT, respite care, social skills treatment, parent/advocacy resources
- **Autism Speaks 100 Day Kit** — Roadmap for community-based intervention services ([autismspeaks.org](http://www.autismspeaks.org))
- **Disability Rights California** — Advocacy resource ([disabilityrightsca.org](http://www.disabilityrightsca.org))
- CHLA referrals noted as primary local resource; OC-area resources available on request

## Recommended Reading

- *Autism Spectrum Disorders: What Every Parent Needs to Know* — American Academy of Pediatrics (Rosenblatt & Carbone)
- *An Early Start for Your Child with Autism* — Rogers, Dawson, Vismara
- *Raising a Child with Autism: A Guide to ABA for Parents* — Shira Richman
- *Educating Children with Autism* — National Academy of Science

> Books are supplementary only; implementation should be under direction of a licensed psychologist with expertise in behavior management.

## Adult-Specific Adaptations (Gap Note)

The document explicitly flags the absence of adult-specific recommendations and suggests adaptations for this population. See [[concepts/autism-adult-support]] for cross-document synthesis on this topic.

- ABA principles adapted for adult skill-building
- Social skills groups for workplace and relationship contexts
- CBT adapted for autism
- Vocational rehabilitation services for workplace accommodations
- OT for sensory sensitivities and daily living

## Follow-up and Monitoring

- Reassessment every **12–18 months**
- Ongoing adjustment of interventions based on progress data
- Continuous monitoring of social, emotional, and academic development
- Consistency across home, school, and therapy settings emphasized as critical
